A top-down stealth heist game: 5 heists, 18 guards, 33 treasures, 4 gadgets.

Play as **Kestrel**, a thief working the night city of Aurelune. Slip past
raycast vision cones, hide in shadow and tall grass, throw coins to lure
guards away, douse lamps, and vanish through the green exit hatch with the
prize — unseen.

**Heists:** The Lantern Quay · The Paper Lantern Theatre · The Aurelune Bank ·
The Museum of Small Hours · The Gilded Spire.

**Controls:** WASD / arrows to move · Shift walk · Ctrl/C sneak · Space sprint ·
Q throw a coin · E smoke bomb · R douse a lamp · F flash powder · M mute · P pause.

Open `game/index.html` in a browser to play. `?autopilot=1` plays all five
heists by itself. `?shot=cover|spotted|lure|win` stages a screenshot scene.
